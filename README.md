# TomTom Traffic Analysis 2024

## Project Overview

This project explores urban traffic conditions worldwide using data from the **TomTom Traffic Index 2024**. The goal was to compare travel times and congestion levels across cities and identify the places facing the greatest traffic challenges.

The analysis focuses particularly on **Colombia**, whose cities rank high in the global ranking. The dashboard moves from a global overview, through a comparison of Colombia with Europe and Poland, to a detailed analysis of five Colombian cities.

## Dataset

The analysis is based on the **TomTom Traffic Index 2024**, covering **501 cities worldwide**.

The dataset includes:

* world ranking,
* average travel time per 10 km,
* congestion level,
* hours lost in traffic per year,
* change in travel time compared with 2023.

## Dashboard Structure

The dashboard consists of **three pages**, moving from a global perspective to a detailed analysis of Colombia.

### 1. Global Traffic Ranking

The first page provides an overview of traffic conditions around the world and highlights cities ranking highest in the TomTom Traffic Index 2024.

An interactive map shows the geographic distribution of the highest-ranked cities and allows their key traffic indicators to be compared.

### 2. Colombia vs Europe & Poland

The second page focuses on comparing **Colombia, Europe and Poland**.

It highlights differences in average travel time per 10 km and compares Colombia with high-ranking cities from Europe and Poland, including London and Wrocław.

### 3. Colombian Cities - Deep Dive

The final page provides a closer look at five Colombian cities:

**Barranquilla, Cartagena, Bogotá, Cali and Medellín.**

The cities can be compared by:

* travel time per 10 km,
* congestion level,
* hours lost in traffic,
* world ranking,
* change compared with 2023.

A dynamic selector allows users to switch between key traffic indicators within a single visual, while custom tooltips provide additional details for each city.

The page also compares Colombia's average travel time with the corresponding averages for Europe and Poland.

## Key Insights

* Colombian cities rank high in the global TomTom traffic ranking.
* **Barranquilla** has the longest average travel time per 10 km among the analyzed Colombian cities.
* Average travel time in Colombia is considerably longer than the averages for Europe and Poland.
* Travel times improved compared with 2023 in most of the analyzed Colombian cities.
* **Bogotá** was the only analyzed city where travel time increased compared with the previous year.
* **Cali** recorded the largest improvement among the analyzed cities.

## Dashboard Features

The project includes:
